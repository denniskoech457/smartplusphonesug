Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum age to apply?⌃

Applicants must be 22 years or older to qualify for our installment plan.

How long does it take to receive my phone?⌃

After payment confirmation, your phone is typically ready for collection or delivery within 2–3 business days.

What happens if I miss a payment?⌃

If you miss a payment, your device may be temporarily locked. We'll contact you immediately to arrange payment. Contact our support team for assistance.

Can I choose weekly payments instead of daily?⌃

Yes. Select your preferred daily, weekly, or monthly payment frequency and choose a 3, 6, 9, or 12-month plan when submitting your order.

Is the deposit refundable?⌃

The deposit is credited towards your total phone cost. It is not refundable but counts as your first payment.

Can I change my phone after applying?⌃

Yes, you can change your phone selection before payment confirmation. Contact our support team for assistance.
